# Built-in skills

Copied into every new workspace once (`acf.skills.seed`); users can change or delete them.

`action-items`, `cited-research`, `pdf`, `plain-writing`, `price-watch` and `weekly-review` are short
rewrites, for SadSha AI Agent's tools, of skills from Hermes Agent by Nous Research
(https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2025 Nous Research). The
guidance in `skill-creator` on what makes a good skill follows the same project's skill-review prompts.

`council-of-llms` is original to SadSha AI Agent: it runs on the `agent` tool (sub-agents).

`personal-assistant` is original to SadSha AI Agent; its triage and brief follow common executive-assistant
practice (delete / delegate / defer / do, a one-screen brief of only what needs the user's judgment).
